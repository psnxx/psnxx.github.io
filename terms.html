What Clipware is

Clipware is a personal tool that makes short clips from livestreams and posts them to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to other users.

Use of platforms

Clipware posts only to accounts the owner signed in to and only content the owner chose to publish. The owner is responsible for following each platform's own terms and community guidelines, and for having the right to post each clip.
