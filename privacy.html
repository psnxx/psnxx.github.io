Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who uses Clipware

Clipware is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer, with the owner's own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok accounts.

What data it uses

When the owner signs in with Google, Clipware receives permission to upload videos to the owner's YouTube channel and to read that channel's video statistics (views, likes, comments and watch time). It uses this only to post clips and to see how they perform. When the owner signs in with TikTok, Clipware receives permission to read the account's basic profile and to publish videos to it, and uses this only to post clips.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token is stored in Windows Credential Manager on the owner's computer. Clip statistics are stored in a local file on the same computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's, Meta's, TikTok's and Twitch's own services, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and for TikTok in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services. Deleting the app's local files removes all stored data.
